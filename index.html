Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about TaxFlow AI for your chartered accountancy practice.

How does TaxFlow AI ingest WhatsApp invoices automatically? +

We provide your CA firm with a dedicated WhatsApp Business Cloud API number. Your clients or their accountants simply click a photo or forward invoice PDFs. Our AI Vision engine immediately extracts the GSTIN, invoice number, dates, line items, and tax amounts, mapping it directly into your purchase ledger.

Does TaxFlow integrate directly with TallyPrime and Zoho Books? +

Yes! TaxFlow features an automated bi-directional WebSocket bridge that syncs extracted purchase invoices directly into your local TallyPrime XML port (9000) or Zoho Books via REST APIs with zero manual data entry.

How legally valid are the AI-drafted ASMT-11 responses? +

The legal engine is tuned on statutory GST jurisprudence, citing Section 16(2), Section 75(4) personal hearing rights, and landmark High Court precedents (like D.Y. Beathel Enterprises). Every draft is prepared for CA review and includes auto-generated reconciliation tables in Annexure-A before final submission.

Is client tax data secure and confidential? +

Completely. All data in transit is encrypted using 256-bit TLS 1.3, and resting data resides in isolated PostgreSQL schemas per firm. We never train public AI models on your clients' proprietary financial records.
